What is the show about?

Cannibal Charlie follows our main character Charlie . Being the outcast of her community, she puts all of her attention and focus into her studies of becoming a detective. After the town's constant talk of a near by circus, she begins to look into the matter.

One or two coincidences are fine to brush off. Right? Except it's more than a few, too many to ignore. But others are. It's up to Lucinda to bring this to everyone's attention.

Will she be able to convince them? Or is it simply nothing she is trying to desperately make something out of?

How did the show start?

The FunTime Show started in fall of 2021. It began when I started practicing drawing people with masks. Soon after, I began designing characters with creative and complex designs. This sparked me to create a story, combining this with my recent interest in circus's, clowns, and carnivals; And the idea was born.

TFTS was originally meant to be a comic with a completely different story. In the beginning, it was about a teenage girl who escaped her bullies into the deep forest, coming across an abandoned circus. Little did she know that circus was alive. This premise was scrapped though, as it sounded a bit to cliché for my liking. I also wanted to make something a bit more exciting yet keep that mysterious enviroment.

The story overall has been scrapped and redone around 6 times .
